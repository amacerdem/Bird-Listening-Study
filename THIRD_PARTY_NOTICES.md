# Source material and licenses

The study implementation is Amaç Erdem's research software. No general
open-source license is granted by this private repository.

- Blackbird recording: Oona Räisänen, 2007, common blackbird (Turdus merula), public
  domain (PD-self). https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turdus_merula_2.ogg
- Flute samples: VSCO 2 Community Edition, CC0.
  https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
- Piano samples: Alexander Holm, Salamander Grand Piano v3, CC BY 3.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Study audio is transformed, resynthesized, pitch-shifted where applicable,
  tapered, converted to mono and level-matched. The manifest identifies the
  original source and exact rendered clips.
- The preserved MI-STM interface includes Inter and Three.js. Their license
  notices are retained in `public/mi-stm/assets/inter-license.txt` and
  `public/mi-stm/vendor/license`.
- npm dependencies retain their own package licenses.
